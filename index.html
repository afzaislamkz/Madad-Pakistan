ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

Serving Citizens Nationwide

MADAD is a community-first civic initiative created to bridge the gap between citizens in distress and verified response hotlines, by centralising government, private and NGO emergency contacts across Pakistan into one fast, mobile-friendly directory.

MADAD IS NOT A GOVERNMENT WEBSITE

  • • MADAD is not run, funded by, or affiliated with the Government of Pakistan or any provincial government.
  • • MADAD is not an emergency dispatch centre — it cannot send police, ambulance or rescue teams. It only displays the number to call.
  • • In immediate danger, call the number directly. Don't wait for a reply from this website, and don't submit a "Report" form instead of calling.
  • • Private hospitals and NGO-run ambulance services (e.g. Edhi, Chhipa) are included for public convenience — they are independent organisations, not government services.

How MADAD verifies information

Verified — the number was checked against that organisation's own official website or a government portal, and the source is shown on the card.

Verification Needed — the number is publicly and widely known, but MADAD hasn't yet confirmed it directly against a primary official source. It's still shown — MADAD never hides or invents numbers — but treat it with a little more caution, and use a search engine to double check if you have a moment and it's not urgent.

An alternative number is only shown if it came from the same verified official source as the primary number — MADAD does not add alternative numbers it can't confirm.

MADAD does not invent or guess numbers. If we can't confirm something, we say so clearly instead of hiding the gap.
